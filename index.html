FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Straight answers. No spin.

What's an MVP and do I actually need one?
MVP stands for Minimum Viable Product — the simplest version of your business that can make money. You don't need a perfect website, a full product line, or a polished brand on day one. You need to validate that someone will pay for what you're offering. That's what we build first.
Which AI tools will we use?
It depends on your business — but there are AI tools that can replace design software, copywriting tools, customer service platforms, scheduling apps, and more. I'll recommend what's actually useful for your specific situation, not a generic list of tools everyone talks about.
Do I need to subscribe to Shopify or other paid platforms?
Not necessarily — and definitely not on day one. Part of what we figure out in our first session is the leanest possible tech stack for what you're building. Some businesses need Shopify, others can start for free or near-free on other platforms. We'll find your path before you spend a dollar on subscriptions.
I have zero experience. Can you really help me?
Yes — complete beginners are who I work with best. No business background, no tech skills, no fully formed idea required. The only thing you need is a willingness to show up and do the work between our sessions.
What if I'm not happy with the first session?
I'll refund you in full. No awkward conversation required. I'd rather you walk away with your money than stick around feeling like it wasn't worth it.
Will you build my website or write my content for me?
I won't — but I'll sit with you while you do it and show you exactly how. I'll also show you which AI tools make it much easier than you think. The goal is that you own everything and know how to run it yourself when we're done.
How does booking work?
Click any "Book" button, pick a time, and pay securely via Stripe. You'll get an instant confirmation email with a calendar invite and video call link. Fully automated — no back-and-forth until we actually meet.
